# Glossy transit direction

The user rejected the restrained paper/vermillion v3. Their explicit direction is glassy, glossy, maximalist and eye-catching. Rains and BOC were inspected in the browser after they supplied those references.

## Reference extraction

- Rains: full-bleed campaign imagery, oversized short headlines over photography, compact floating navigation. Adopted immersive imagery and small glass navigation; not its retail content.
- BOC work: dense horizontal project-image sequences, sparse framing and a persistent information rail. Adopted visual gallery rhythm and scrolling narrative; not its brand assets.
- Generated hero: split typography left, train nose right; two-line white/lime headline, short supporting copy, two contrasting controls. Midnight #040d20, cyan #53ddff, lime #d7ff79, silver surfaces. Code uses the fresh text-free artwork, not a flattened screenshot.
- Features: 2+1+1 column proportions, three occupied panels, 12px gutters, 20–22px corners, glass edge highlights. Native buttons provide the accordion, hover on capable pointers, click and keyboard elsewhere. No invented statistics.
- Story: left title pins on wide screens; right reflective panels move with scroll. A word reveal supplies the second selected GSAP paradigm. Phone layout becomes a normal vertical sequence.
- CTA: large centered two-line copy, bright primary action, quieter reward/messaging links. Fresh route artwork supplies reflections. Removed invented labels, slogans and logos introduced in the reference image.
- Planner: the reference is illustrative. Only its dark palette, glass surfaces and hierarchy are implemented. No generated fares, realtime claims or fake map content are copied. Actual Google map uses dark styling with cyan selected routes and grey alternatives.

RNG: seed 103; Editorial Split, Geist; Horizontal Accordions, Infinite Marquee, Inline Typography Images; Scroll Pinning, Scrubbing Text Reveals.

## Artwork

Built-in image generation produced five separate section references and three original text-free assets. References are hero.png, features.png, story.png, cta.png, planner.png.
