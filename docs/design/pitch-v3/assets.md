- Source page explicitly offers its public **Embed** function. The page uses the official oEmbed preview and links to that official hosted viewer, with its native viewer UI and creator identity intact. It does not download, copy, extract, modify, or self-host the paid asset.
- This is not a CC0 or Creative Commons asset. No purchase or downloadable-asset license was obtained. Do not redistribute its geometry or textures.
- Visible attribution links to the model, SQUIR3D, and names Sketchfab. The linked viewer retains its own attribution and controls; no UI-hiding parameters are applied.
- Public model statistics: 730.1k triangles, 408.9k vertices. Download size is unavailable because the model is not offered as a free download.
- Visually verified in the official browser viewer: realistic two-car NYC R160 exterior, detailed undercarriage and rails, textured metal, route signage, flags, and MTA markings. The publisher describes it as a high-detail model.
- Access checked during this task. Playback uses a light silver studio environment. Users explicitly open the official viewer to orbit and zoom; the pitch artwork is static and does not autoplay, including for reduced-motion users.
- The optional `progress` component prop is retained for compatibility. It does not animate the preview or fake train motion.
